About Us

Greenwich's Most Trusted Child Care Since 2009

Founded by early childhood education specialists, Greenwich Little Learners was built on a simple belief: every child deserves a safe, joyful, and enriching start. Located in the heart of Greenwich, CT, we've been a trusted partner for local families for over 15 years.

Our mission is to nurture the whole child — intellectually, socially, emotionally, and physically — in a warm community where every learner feels seen, valued, and inspired.

Safety First

Secure entry systems, CPR-trained staff, and rigorous protocols so parents feel confident every day.

Nurturing Care

Small groups and attentive educators mean your child is truly known and loved.

Expert Educators

All lead teachers hold ECE degrees and participate in ongoing professional development.

Inclusive Community

We celebrate diverse backgrounds and foster empathy, kindness, and respect.

Programs

Age-Appropriate Programs Designed to Inspire

Our play-based curriculum follows the CT ELDS framework and is tailored to each developmental stage.

Infant Care

6 Weeks – 12 Months

Our infant room is a calm, cozy haven designed around your baby's unique schedule. We focus on attachment, sensory exploration, and responsive caregiving.

  • 1:4 teacher-to-infant ratio
  • Personalized daily schedules
  • Tummy time & sensory play
  • Daily digital progress reports
  • Organic, puréed meals
🧸

Toddler Program

1 – 3 Years

Toddlers are natural explorers. Our program channels their boundless energy into language development, social skills, creative arts, and motor milestones.

  • 1:6 teacher ratio
  • Language & literacy focus
  • Music, movement & art
  • Outdoor play daily
  • Potty training support
🎓

Pre-K Academy

3 – 5 Years

Our flagship Pre-K program builds a strong academic foundation alongside social-emotional skills — ensuring children are confident and ready for kindergarten.

  • 1:8 teacher ratio
  • Reading & math readiness
  • STEM exploration projects
  • Spanish & music classes
  • Kindergarten readiness assessment

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your hours of operation?
We are open Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM, year-round with the exception of major holidays and a brief summer training week.
Do you accept subsidy or financial assistance?
Yes! We accept Care4Kids (CT State Subsidy) and work with many employer-sponsored dependent care programs. Contact us to discuss your options.
What is your sick child policy?
To protect all children and staff, we ask that sick children remain home. Children must be fever-free for 24 hours without medication before returning. We follow CT DPH guidelines.
Is there a waitlist?
Our infant and toddler rooms often have waitlists. We recommend applying 3–6 months before your desired start date. Pre-K spots open more frequently.
Do you accommodate food allergies?
Absolutely. We are a nut-free facility and our kitchen staff is trained in allergen management. We work closely with families to create a safe meal plan for every child.
